FAQ

Questions students ask us most

Can’t find your answer? Our counsellors reply within one working day.

Ask on WhatsApp
Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first consultation — in person, by phone or on Zoom — is completely free with no obligation.

How much does it cost to study abroad?

It depends on the country, course and city. As a guide, annual tuition ranges from almost nothing at German public universities to around €10,000–€30,000 in Ireland, with Finland and Sweden in between (often reduced by scholarships). Malaysia is the most affordable overall. We give you a full, honest cost breakdown before you apply.

What qualifications do I need?

Most bachelor’s programmes accept good A/L results; foundation and diploma pathways are available (especially in Malaysia) if you do not meet direct entry. Master’s programmes require a recognised bachelor’s degree or equivalent.

Do I need IELTS or PTE?

Usually, yes — but some universities accept other evidence of English or run their own tests. We tell you exactly which test and score you need.

When should I start my application?

Ideally 8–12 months before your intake. Finland’s joint application opens in January and Sweden’s closes in mid-January for the autumn intake, so early planning matters.

Can I work while studying?

Yes. Sweden has no fixed weekly limit, Finland allows about 30 hours a week, Ireland 20 hours in term, and Germany 140 full days a year. Malaysia allows work only during semester breaks. See each destination page for details.

Can my spouse or children come with me?

It depends on the country and course level. Germany and Sweden are generally more open; Ireland usually does not allow student dependants. We assess your family’s eligibility during the consultation.

Do you help after I arrive?

Yes. We help with accommodation, airport pick-up guidance and stay in touch through your first semester.
