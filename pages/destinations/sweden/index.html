Study in Sweden

Sweden is home to world-class research universities and global companies, and it has a culture built on innovation, sustainability and equality. Thousands of English-taught master’s programmes make it a strong option for ambitious Sri Lankan graduates.

Why study in Sweden?

Innovation hub

The birthplace of companies such as Spotify, IKEA and Volvo, with strong links between universities and industry.

Student-centred teaching

Small groups, independent thinking and close contact with lecturers.

No limit on work hours

Students on a residence permit can work part-time with no fixed weekly hour limit, as long as studies come first.

Scholarships available

Swedish Institute scholarships and university tuition waivers can cover most or all costs.

Visa overview

After paying your first tuition instalment, apply online to the Swedish Migration Agency for a residence permit for studies. You need your admission letter, proof of funds for the full permit period and comprehensive health insurance (if your course is shorter than one year). Apply early — processing can take several weeks.

Work rights

During studies
Part-time work allowed with no fixed hour limit — studies must remain your main activity
After graduation
Residence permit to look for work or start a business — up to 12 months
Dependants
Partners and children may be eligible, subject to income and housing requirements

Frequently asked questions

When should I apply for Sweden?

For the autumn intake, applications open in mid-October and close in mid-January at universityadmissions.se. We recommend starting your documents by September.

Can I get a scholarship in Sweden?

Yes. The Swedish Institute Scholarships for Global Professionals and many university tuition-fee waivers are open to eligible Sri Lankan applicants. Deadlines are often around February.
