UK vs Australia: which is right for Sri Lankan students in 2027?

Course length, costs, work rights and post-study options compared side by side.

The UK and Australia remain the two most popular study destinations for Sri Lankan students. Both offer world-class universities and English-medium teaching, but they differ in ways that matter for your budget and your long-term plans.

Course length and total cost

UK taught master’s degrees usually last 12 months, while Australian master’s programmes are typically 1.5–2 years. That makes the UK cheaper overall for many postgraduate students, even though annual tuition can look similar.

Work during and after study

Australia allows up to 48 hours of work per fortnight during term, compared with 20 hours a week in the UK. After graduation, Australia’s Temporary Graduate visa can run for 2–3 years or more, while the UK Graduate Route is currently 2 years.

Bringing your family

If you plan to bring a spouse or children, Australia is generally more flexible. The UK now restricts dependants to postgraduate research students.

Our advice

There is no single “best” country. Choose based on your course, budget, family situation and career goals — and talk to a counsellor who will compare both honestly.
