How Finland’s joint application works — a step-by-step guide

Key dates, entrance exams and documents for the autumn 2027 intake.

Most English-taught bachelor’s programmes in Finland are open for applications at the same time, through one national portal.

Key dates

The main joint application for autumn programmes opens in early January and stays open for about two weeks. Results are usually published in spring.

Entrance exams

Many universities of applied sciences use an online entrance exam, sometimes followed by an interview. Practising with past exam formats makes a big difference.

Documents

Prepare certified copies of your O/L and A/L results, your passport and proof of English early, as some must be uploaded before the deadline.
